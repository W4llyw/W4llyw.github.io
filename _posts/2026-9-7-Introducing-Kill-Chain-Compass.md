I wanted a tool I could use to help quickly look up threat actors and associate malware samples and IOCs with them. 

So I made a site: [Kill Chain Compass](https://kcc.w4llysw0rld.com) 
![Site](assets/img/kcc/site.png)
The site was built primarily for my own interest in threat hunting and actor profiling, but it ended up turning into a useful resource. I showed it to some friends, who also mentioned it could be helpful during investigations. This made me consider smaller companies that might not be able to afford advanced tooling but could find themselves wanting for much needed information when they are targeted by a threat actor.
